## Research highlight

**How much do bar backtests overstate results?** We ran 102 strategies on 421 instruments, filled on daily, 1-minute and second-level data. Of the daily-bar backtests that looked profitable with the optimistic "target first" convention, **51%** were not profitable once every order was filled second by second (stops at the trigger second's price). For intraday strategies on 1-minute bars it was **60%**.
→ [Method and data](https://github.com/matanalabs/research/tree/main/bar-resolution-backtest-inflation) · [Full article](https://matanalabs.com/research/bar-resolution-backtest-inflation/)

## The lab

- **Backtest Lab**: up to 23 years of history, every fill checked second by second.
- **Analysis Lab**: 31 automated robustness checks roll up into a score out of 100.
- **Optimization Lab**: parameter search, with the best variants checked on days the search never saw.
- **Prop Lab**: prop challenge practice with the firm's rules checked on every tick, and your odds to pass.
- **Strategy Lab**: build without code, or describe the idea to Matana AI in plain words.
- **Chart Lab**, **Market Lab**, **Journal Lab**: charts and alerts, watchlists and a screener, a journal for your real trades.

Free plan available. Runs in the browser, nothing to install.
