# Matana Labs

**Test your idea before you risk a dollar.**

Matana Labs is a trading lab in your browser: backtests filled second by second on up to 23 years of history, 31 robustness checks rolled into one score out of 100, prop challenge odds from your own backtest, and an AI that builds strategies from plain words.

### What we publish here

- **Research** — methodology and results of our backtest studies (what works, what breaks, and why)
- **Open datasets** — prop firm challenge rules, kept up to date
- **Tools** — small open-source utilities for traders
